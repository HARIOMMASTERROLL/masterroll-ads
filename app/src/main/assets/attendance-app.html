About Us

MASTER ROLL is a professional Attendance and Salary management tool designed for small to medium-sized businesses.

Our mission is to simplify the complex calculations of statutory deductions like PF, ESI, and LWF, providing a transparent and easy-to-use ledger for both employers and employees.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: Aug 2026

1. Data Storage: All data entered into Master Roll, including employee names, salaries, and attendance records, is stored locally on your device. We do not upload your business data to any external servers.

2. Data Security: Since your data stays on your phone, you are responsible for its security. Use the "Backup to File" feature frequently to keep your records safe.

3. Permissions: The app requires storage permissions only to save and restore your backup files and generate PDF slips.

4. Updates: When updating the app via Android Studio, Android usually preserves your data, but always perform a manual backup first.
